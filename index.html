02 A NOTE FROM THE EDGE FIELD NOTE · NO. 014 · UNSENT

It is always
quieter on
the other side.

I found the island just before the fog remembered it was fog. The sky was the colour of an old blue cup. Someone had left a door standing open in a tree.

I didn't go in. Some things feel more like yours when you leave them exactly as you found them.

for another kind of distance.

you can't explain.

II / WANDER⌁

Stay for the
strange weather.

Every place changes if you give it a little time. So does the person looking.

III / NOTICE✳

thing in mind.

A sound. A colour. The way the dark made room for something bright.

IV / RETURN↶

only the feeling.

The best souvenirs fit in the quiet pocket behind your ribs.
